## `whoami`

Hi, I'm **Fridolph**, a frontend / full-stack engineer based in Chengdu, China.

I enjoy building practical products, exploring how humans and AI work together, and turning what I learn into reusable knowledge. My projects bring together frontend engineering, AI-assisted workflows, technical writing, and an ongoing interest in Dao and traditional wisdom.

Here's what I'm currently exploring:

- **Engineering:** building maintainable products with Vue, Nuxt, React, TypeScript, Node.js, NestJS, Python, and modern frontend tooling.
- **AI collaboration:** developing reusable skills and workflows for clearer requirements, handoffs, reviews, and delivery.
- **Knowledge:** organizing learning notes, interview reviews, and project experience into a long-term knowledge garden.
- **Dao:** exploring traditional wisdom, thinking models, and their connection to learning and personal growth.

## Featured Projects

### [`my-resume`](https://github.com/Fridolph/my-resume)

A personal resume system evolving from an online CV into a full-stack personal product.

- **Stack:** `TypeScript` `Next.js` `React` `NestJS` `SQLite` `Tailwind CSS`
- **Live:** [resume.fridolph.top](https://resume.fridolph.top/)
- **Direction:** explore structured resume data and future AI-powered capabilities.

### [`Fridolph`](https://github.com/Fridolph/Fridolph)

A long-term knowledge garden for frontend engineering, JavaScript, TypeScript, Vue, Node.js, AI, and related topics.

- **Format:** `Markdown` `VitePress` `GitHub Pages`
- **Knowledge base:** [fridolph.github.io/Fridolph](https://fridolph.github.io/Fridolph/)
- **Goal:** turn scattered learning into a searchable, maintainable, and reusable knowledge asset.

### [`FE-prepare-interview`](https://github.com/Fridolph/FE-prepare-interview)

A collection of frontend interview topics and reference materials for reviewing concepts and reflecting on different solutions.

- **Live:** [fridolph.github.io/FE-prepare-interview](https://fridolph.github.io/FE-prepare-interview/)
- **Principle:** use references to support thinking and review—not as answers to copy without understanding.

### [`AI-Journey-Fighting`](https://github.com/Fridolph/AI-Journey-Fighting)

A learning repository for AI concepts, LLM fundamentals, and hands-on experiments.

- **Live:** [fridolph.github.io/AI-Journey-Fighting](https://fridolph.github.io/AI-Journey-Fighting/)
- **Focus:** core concepts, model fundamentals, prompt practice, and small experiments.
- **Goal:** document the moments when abstract AI ideas begin to make sense.

### [`Dao`](https://github.com/Fridolph/Dao)

A guide-oriented knowledge site for exploring Dao, Yi Jing, and traditional wisdom.

> 道生一，一生二，二生三，三生万物。  
> 易有太极，是生两仪；两仪生四象，四象生八卦。

- **Live:** [fridolph.github.io/Dao](https://fridolph.github.io/Dao/)
- **Focus:** reading paths, traditional texts, and an accessible starting point for learning.
- **Note:** this project intentionally keeps its language and spirit in Chinese.

### [`Dao Skills`](https://github.com/Fridolph/dao-skills)

A collection of reusable development skills designed to help humans and AI maintain a shared understanding from requirements to delivery.

- **Covers:** requirement analysis, frontend and backend design, API delivery, task alignment, handoffs, reviews, and knowledge sharing.
- **Approach:** install the skills you need and use them alongside your existing development workflow.
- **Goal:** make collaboration across people, models, and sessions more consistent and traceable.
